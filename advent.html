Cryptic Advent 2025

Welcome to Cryptic Advent 2025! This is a 24-day cryptic crossword puzzle suitable for total beginners and experienced solvers of cryptic crosswords.

Daily, 3-4 new cryptic clues will be released without any information on length or location... those details become available the following day. Two days after a clue releases, you will get some hints on how to solve it. This is heavily inspired by (i.e. I've blantantly stolen the idea of) Daniel Peake's PUZZLEVENT 2024. My thanks to AP, JH, JA and FS for contributing some guest clues for this year's puzzle. Please note, I'm a one-person operation so clues may not appear at a regular time! I aim to have clues available by early afternoon each day.

At the end of the event, the first person to send me a completed grid will recieve absolutely nothing.

For my normal cryptics, please see my Crosswords page.
