About Me

I have just completed my PhD in Pure Mathematics at the University of Manchester, supervised by Prof. Mark Kambites and Dr. Nóra Szakács. My thesis title was Graphical Approaches to Left Adequate Monoids and I defended (successfully!) on the 18th December 2025.

My pronouns are currently They/Them.

I'm a part of Manchester's Semigroups Group, particularly interested in semigroup connections with combinatorics and graph theory, but I can also be found working with complexity theory, decision problems, geometric (semi)group theory, groupoids, and cancellative and inverse monoids with an eye to C*-algebras. My Erdős number is 4.

When not staring at graphs, I am a dysgwyr Cymraeg, hobbyist programmer, variant sudokuer and table tennis player-coach. I am also an amateur cruciverbalist and have made a range of Cryptic Crosswords which you are free to have a look at.

I'm always excited to hear about interesting maths, especially semigroups. I'm available to be a reviewer also. Feel free to contact me. Dw i'n hapus i geisio siarad yn Gymraeg...!
